Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexDown. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the NexDown Desktop Application and Website. We built NexDown with privacy and security in mind.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information.

  • We do not track your browsing history or activity.
  • We do not log or store data about the videos or audio you download.
  • All download processing is handled locally on your own machine.
  • No user accounts, sign-ups, or personal credentials are required.

3. Local Device Storage

NexDown Desktop saves your requested media files directly to your chosen local download directory (default: ~/Downloads/NexDown). It does not upload your files to any remote server.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party tracking, profiling, or intrusive advertising tools. Your usage remains completely private.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
