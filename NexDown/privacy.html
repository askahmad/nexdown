Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexDown. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the NexDown Chrome Extension. We built NexDown with privacy in mind.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information.

  • We do not track your browsing history.
  • We do not collect data about the videos you download.
  • All processing is done locally within your browser.

3. Permissions

NexDown requires certain permissions to function correctly. These permissions are strictly used to enable the downloading functionality:

  • downloads: Required to save the media files to your local storage.
  • activeTab: Required to detect downloadable media on the page you are currently viewing.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools within the extension. Your usage remains completely private.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
