# NexDown

**NexDown** is a powerful Chrome extension that lets you download videos from anywhere with a single click. Fast, secure, and completely free.

## Features

- **⚡ Lightning Fast**: Optimized download engine ensures you get your files in seconds, not minutes. Maximizes your bandwidth automatically.
- **🎥 Up to 4K Quality**: Don't settle for blurry pixels. NexDown fetches the highest available resolution for crystal clear offline viewing.
- **🛡️ 100% Secure**: We respect your privacy. No data collection, no intrusive ads, and no background tracking. Just a clean tool.

## How it Works

1. **Install Extension**: Add NexDown to your Chrome browser with a single click from the official Web Store.
2. **Find Your Video**: Navigate to any webpage containing the video you want to save to your device.
3. **Click & Download**: Click the NexDown icon in your toolbar and select your preferred quality to start downloading.
